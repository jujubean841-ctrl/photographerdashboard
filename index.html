WHY I WANT TO JOIN

Let me help tell our school's story.

I want to join the Yearbook Club because I think photography would be a fun opportunity to try something new, learn from other people, and help capture memories at school.

I feel like I have a good eye for pictures, and I want to see how much better I can get with practice. I'd love to take photos at school events, capture special moments, and help make a yearbook that students can look back on for years.

I'm willing to learn, try new things, and put real effort into making my pictures look great. I think this club would be a great way to build my photography skills while being part of something at school.
